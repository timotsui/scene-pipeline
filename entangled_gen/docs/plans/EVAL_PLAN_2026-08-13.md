## THE FRAME

GLTS (CVPR 2025) and Holodeck (CVPR 2024) both evaluate ONLY on
plausibility — top-down renders scored by CLIP and by human preference —
because text-to-scene has no ground truth to compare against. Our
setting does: we reconstruct a CAPTURED world, so the capture itself is
the reference. The evaluation therefore has two kinds of axes, kept
explicitly separate (comparison.html's honesty note already does this):

- COMPARABLE axes: computable identically for both methods.
- ASYMMETRIC axes: only defined in our setting — these are the
  contribution, not a rigged scorecard, and the text must say so.

## WHAT THE BASELINES USE (from the papers, verified in the PDFs)

- GLTS: (1) CLIP score — top-down Blender render vs "a top-down view of
  [scene type]", OpenCLIP ViT-L/14 (LAION-2B), cosine × 100;
  (2) reciprocal rank by 15 human annotators on shuffled top-down pairs.
  Nothing else. 120 ChatGPT prompts, 4 residential room types.
- Holodeck: the same CLIP score (GLTS borrowed it); pairwise human
  preference vs ProcTHOR (asset selection / layout coherence / overall);
  absolute 1–5 human ratings on 52 scene types (top-down + 360° video);
  human MRR over layout ablations (criteria: out-of-boundary, collision,
  reachable space, realism); embodied-navigation Success/SPL.

## OUR METRICS

### Comparable (both sides, automatic)

1. **CLIP score, their exact recipe.** Top-down render of our final GLB
   and of GLTS's layout render, same prompt template, OpenCLIP
   ViT-L/14. The one number reviewers will look for. TODO: script.
2. **Physics counts.** Out-of-bounds, collisions, floating objects,
   computed the same way on both sides. §4.1 RULED 08-13 (user): count
   the SETTLED MESH itself (the final GLB), never the boxes — "we know
   the box is sometimes crazy". Final product vs final product.
   compare_methods.py's physics rows must be repointed at the GLB
   before the sheet rebuild; the raw-box count MAY appear as a
   clearly-labeled secondary stat (how much work the settle pass does).
3. **Wall-clock time + LLM calls.** GLTS: from its own glts_run.json /
   log recount (already filed: 94–150 min, 147–234 calls per scene).
   Ours: time from run records; calls RETRO-COUNTED from per-stage
   receipts (rotation_check "44/45 answered", per-node vote judgments,
   judge verdict files) — a stated lower bound, same recount-from-log
   spirit as the GLTS number. Stated honestly: the two systems do
   different work (we reconstruct + verify; they invent), so time is
   context, not a race. TODO: retro-count probe on one scene first.

### Asymmetric (ours only — fidelity vs the captured world)

4. **Room area / dimension error.** GLTS guesses room size from text;
   we measure the shell. Already on the sheet: GLTS −57%…+2.3%, worst
   on the two-room scene (plaster_bedroom).
5. **Object count: measured vs invented.** Ours 15–60 evidence-backed
   boxes per scene; GLTS invents 7–10 objects.
6. **Furniture retention.** GLTS silently loses 1–11 of its OWN
   retrieved items per scene (its search discards them); our gaps are
   recorded in the layer, never hidden.
7. **(Optional) image-to-image CLIP fidelity.** Our top-down render vs
   the ORIGINAL world's top-down splat render — "is this the same
   room?", a question text-to-scene cannot ask. Build only if cheap.

### The realization funnel (ours only — THE STATED LIMITATION)

8. **Realization rate + reason breakdown.** Per scene: measured boxes →
   realized (placed asset in the GLB) → unrealized, itemized by reason:
   no acceptable retrieval match, size-bar rejection, fit_feedback
   complaint (already on disk, unconsumed by design — user ruling
   2026-08-11B), sub-tier deferral. THE POINT (user, verbatim intent):
   be very clear that our system is mostly limited by the ASSET
   LIBRARY, which is swappable and out of scope — the
   measurement/perception side is rich; the furnishing ceiling is
   external. Contrast: when GLTS can't realize an item it silently
   drops it; we show every unrealized box with its evidence and reason.

### Optional replacement for the missing humans

9. **VLM-as-judge pairwise preference.** We cannot recruit humans
   (user ruling today). If a preference study ships at all, it is a
   VLM judging shuffled top-down pairs on Holodeck's three questions,
   fixed prompt, N repeats with agreement reported, labeled "VLM
   preference, not human preference" in the text.

## DELIBERATELY NOT USED

- Human studies of any kind (pairwise, 1–5, reciprocal rank) — no
  recruiting. Their role is covered by CLIP + optional VLM judge.
- Embodied navigation Success/SPL — needs simulator integration;
  future work.
