the design trade

Coverage + votes, not careful geometry

24 / 90 / 192 wide-angle views from 3–8 standpoints inside the scene, then statistical fusion: an object exists only if enough frames voted for it. Per-detection geometry is crude — one surface point and a fabricated depth extent. The exact opposite trade to our path (4 views, careful mask lift).

silent caveats

What its numbers don't tell you

Assumes y-down file frame — a y-up splat renders every frame upside-down with no error (our RAW frame matches; verified in R2). position = front-surface centroid, not volume center; z-extent fabricated as (w+h)/2; rotation always identity; hard cap 3 objects per label.

worth borrowing · OBSERVE evidence

Three portable pieces

The standpoint sampler (a2 — CPU-only, renderer-independent), the 360° az×el grid (a3), and the two-sided vote filter (a7) all work without OWLv2. Direct answers to our 300°/360° hole and "one cross-view merge" weakness — evidence for the open OBSERVE and FIND decisions.
